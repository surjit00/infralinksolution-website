The challenge

The IT Challenges Businesses Face

Your business depends on its data and technology every day. Without a reliable backup and recovery plan, even a single unexpected incident can cause major disruption.

  • Important business data being lost because of hardware or system failures
  • Backups that are incomplete, outdated, or difficult to restore
  • Cybersecurity incidents that affect access to important files and systems
  • Lack of a clear recovery plan when an unexpected disruption occurs
  • Growing amounts of business data that become harder to protect and manage
